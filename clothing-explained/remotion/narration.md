# Coin pocket, 30 second narrator script

Strongest hook, from hooks.md: "Your tiny jeans pocket was built for a pocket watch, not coins?" (12 words)
Verified fact only: the tiny pocket was originally a watch pocket, made to hold a pocket watch securely and protect it from tools and dust in the main pocket. Levi Strauss still calls it the watch pocket. Sources: Gear Patrol, https://www.gearpatrol.com/style/jeans-watch-pocket/ ; Levi Strauss, https://www.levistrauss.com/2022/10/12/countdown-to-150-501-fab-facts/

No dates, no extra numbers, no invented detail. About 70 words total, paced near 2.3 words per second so the narration fits 30 seconds without rushing.

## Timed lines

- 0 to 5s: "Your tiny jeans pocket was built for a pocket watch, not coins?"
- 5 to 15s: "It was a watch pocket. The watch slid in snug, safe in its own little case."
- 15 to 25s: "Your tools and dust stayed in the main pocket, while the watch stayed protected."
- 25 to 30s: "So that tiny pocket was built to keep a watch safe." (spoken while the pocket snaps back, ending mid-motion, no closing claim beyond the verified fact)

## Sound effect cues, one per visible movement

- 0s: whoosh as the pocket lifts off, camera already orbiting.
- 5s: soft metallic clink as the watch appears, whoosh as it slides in.
- 12s: click as the watch settles snug in the pocket.
- 15s: fabric rustle and soft thunks as tools slide into the main pocket.
- 20s: faint rustle as dust drifts.
- 25s: whoosh, then a snap as the pocket clicks back into place.
- 30s: cut mid-motion, no fade out, no music.
