# Clothing Explained: Levi's blank red tab

Topic chosen by the Choose the Topic skill. Verified fact: a blank red tab is deliberate, about 1 in 10 Levi's products carry a blank tab, to keep the trademark in the tab's shape and placement itself in continuous use, so Levi's can stop copycats who copy the red tab but change the name.

Guess: a blank tab is a misprint, factory defect, or fake.
Truth: deliberate trademark protection, in the shape and placement, not the name.

Mechanism chain, verified facts only:
1. In 1936 Levi's sewed a small red cloth tab into the right back-pocket seam to identify genuine jeans at a glance.
2. Levi's registered not only the "LEVI'S" word on the tab but the tab's shape and placement itself, so a copycat using a different name on a similar red tab can still infringe.
3. A trademark can be lost if it is not used for more than five years, because someone can file a cancellation action and the protection becomes open for public use.
4. So Levi's puts a blank tab, only the ®, no name, on about 10% of its products, mixing blank tabs through its assortment.
5. The blank tab therefore proves the shape itself is in use, keeping Levi's able to sue imitators who change the name.
