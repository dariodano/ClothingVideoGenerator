# Clothing Explained: hooks for the two first-video candidates

Prepared 2026-11-05, after Dario's pivot. Main chat already asked him to pick coin pocket or copper rivets; these hook sets are ready for whichever he picks, so no new choice is needed before building.

Rules applied to every hook: 12 words or fewer, names the part in the opening shot within the first few words, about the viewer's own clothes or habits, creates one question and does not answer it, contains one surprise that is true according to the verified fact and nothing more. No generic openers, no invented people, stories, dates, or numbers.

## A. Jeans coin pocket

Verified fact: the tiny pocket was originally a watch pocket, made to hold a pocket watch securely and protect it from tools and dust in the main pocket. Levi Strauss still calls it the watch pocket. Sources checked 2026-11-05: Gear Patrol, https://www.gearpatrol.com/style/jeans-watch-pocket/ ; Levi Strauss, https://www.levistrauss.com/2022/10/12/countdown-to-150-501-fab-facts/

Opening shot: the tiny pocket on the front of the jeans.

1. Your tiny jeans pocket was built for a pocket watch, not coins? (12 words)
2. That tiny pocket on your jeans held a watch before wristwatches existed? (12)
3. Your jeans pocket hides a watch slot, what have you stored there? (12)
4. Check your jeans, that tiny pocket protected a watch from your tools? (12)
5. Your smallest jeans pocket was a watch case, why keep it? (11)
6. That pocket on your jeans was never meant for coins at all? (12)
7. Your jeans tiny pocket kept a watch safe from dust and tools? (12)
8. Your jeans pocket was designed around a watch, not your coins? (11)

Strongest pick: 1. It names the part in the first four words, states the surprise plainly, and the question is the whole hook.

30 second beat sheet, coin pocket:
- 0 to 5s: opening shot on the tiny pocket, hook 1 spoken, pocket lifts off and floats.
- 5 to 15s: a low-poly pocket watch slides into the pocket, snug fit shown, camera keeps orbiting.
- 15 to 25s: tools and dust shown in the main pocket, watch stays protected in its own pocket.
- 25 to 30s: pocket snaps back, end mid-motion, no closing claim beyond the verified fact.

## B. Jeans copper rivets

Verified fact: copper rivets at pocket corners and the base of the fly reinforce stress points and stop pockets tearing off. Patented May 20, 1873 by Jacob Davis and Levi Strauss, U.S. Patent 139,121. Sources checked 2026-11-05: Levi Strauss, https://www.levistrauss.com/2025/03/24/evolution-of-riveted-denim/ ; IPWatchdog patent summary, https://ipwatchdog.com/2014/05/20/today-in-patent-history-blue-jeans-patented-may-20-1873/

Opening shot: a copper rivet at a pocket corner.

1. Those copper rivets on your jeans stop your pockets tearing off? (11 words)
2. Your jeans rivets were patented in 1873, what were they saving? (11)
3. Check your jeans, those tiny rivets hold your pockets together how? (11)
4. Your jeans copper rivets take the strain your pockets cannot? (10)
5. That rivet on your jeans was a patent, not decoration? (10)
6. Your pocket corners have rivets, can you guess what breaks without them? (12)
7. Those rivets on your jeans were invented because pockets kept tearing? (11)
8. Look at your jeans, those rivets stop pockets tearing at the corners? (12)

Strongest pick: 1. It names the part in the first three words, states the job the rivet does, and the question is the whole hook.

30 second beat sheet, copper rivets:
- 0 to 5s: opening shot on a copper rivet at a pocket corner, hook 1 spoken, rivet lifts off and floats.
- 5 to 15s: pocket corner shown pulling and straining, rivet snaps back in and holds it.
- 15 to 25s: rivets shown at the pocket corners and base of the fly, each one clicking into place.
- 25 to 30s: jeans spin and float, end mid-motion, no closing claim beyond the verified fact.
